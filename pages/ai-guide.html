AI Activity Instructions

Adaptive questioning

  1. Ask one question at the student's current level.
  2. Accept voice or text answer.
  3. If incorrect, give a hint rather than the answer immediately.
  4. Allow a second attempt.
  5. Explain the concept briefly.
  6. Increase difficulty after successful responses.

Suggested modes

Learn → Practice → Challenge → Assessment → Project Coach

Safety and classroom use

Teacher remains the learning facilitator. AI supports questioning, hints and practice; it does not replace teacher judgement.
