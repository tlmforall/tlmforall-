Period-wise Teaching Plan

Chapter: Reading and Making Maps | Class: 6 | Subject: Social Studies

  1. Maps: meaning, uses, real-world connection, map observation.
  2. Directions: North, South, East, West and intermediate directions.
  3. Sketch Map: observe a familiar area and represent relative locations.
  4. Scale: connect map distance with actual ground distance.
  5. Symbols: understand legends and create simple symbols.
  6. Complete Map Reading: combine title, direction, scale, symbols and labels.
  7. Revision Game: apply map skills through challenges.
  8. Assessment: recall, understanding, application and map interpretation.
  9. CCE Project: My Neighbourhood Map and presentation.

Teacher Implementation

  • Begin every period with the approved Inspiration Page.
  • Present the SCERT concept before enrichment.
  • Move from explanation to experience and activity.
  • Use the four recurring characters for questioning and discussion.
  • Use AI adaptively: hint → retry → explain → next level.
  • Use Google Earth only where it adds real-world understanding.
