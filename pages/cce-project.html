CLASS 6 • SOCIAL STUDIES • CHAPTER 1

My Neighbourhood Map

Task

Create a simple map of a familiar neighbourhood.

Include

  • Title
  • Direction indicator
  • Suitable scale
  • At least five landmarks
  • Clear symbols and legend
  • Labels
  • One route between two places

Presentation

Explain your map to classmates. Tell them why you selected the area, how you decided directions, and how you used symbols and scale.

Reflection

What was easy? What was difficult? What did you learn?
