🌍 Experience

Imagine travelling from Earth → India → Telangana → your district → your neighbourhood.

A map helps us represent a large real-world area in a form that we can observe and understand.

🎯 Classroom Activity

Think of three places you know. Write how a map could help someone locate or understand each place.

  1. Your village or town
  2. Your school
  3. Your district

👩‍🏫 Teacher Prompt

“If a friend has never visited your village, how will you help them find your school?”
