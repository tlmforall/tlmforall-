✨ Inspiration

“A map is not just a drawing — it is a way to understand a place.”

Did You Know? Maps help people travel, plan, locate places and understand our world.

📖 SCERT Connection

A map is a representation of the whole or a part of the Earth's surface on a flat surface. Maps help us locate and understand places.

🎬 Experience

Imagine travelling from the Earth → India → Telangana → your district → your neighbourhood. A map turns a large real world into information we can understand.
