Sketch Map

Observe → Simplify → Draw

✨ Inspiration

“Learn it. See it. Use it.”

Today’s challenge connects map knowledge with something students can observe around them.

📖 SCERT Connection

A sketch map is a simple representation of places showing relative locations.

🎯 Activity

  1. What places are important?
  2. Where are they in relation to each other?
  3. Which features need symbols or labels?

🌍 Explore the Real World

Where useful, teacher opens Google Earth and connects the concept to a real location. Students compare the real place with a simplified map representation.

🤖 TLM AI

Ask AI for one question on Sketch Map. Student answers by voice or text. Give a hint after an incorrect attempt and increase difficulty after successful answers.
