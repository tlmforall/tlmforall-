CLASS 6 • SOCIAL STUDIES • CHAPTER 1

Class 6 — Chapter 1 Assessment

Time: 45 minutes | Marks: 30

Section A — 5 × 1 = 5

  1. What is a map?
  2. Name the four main directions.
  3. What is a legend?
  4. What does scale show?
  5. What is a sketch map?

Section B — 5 × 1 = 5

  1. Opposite of East?
  2. Name one intermediate direction.
  3. Which element explains symbols?
  4. If 1 cm = 2 km, what is 4 cm?
  5. What is a sketch map?

Section C — 3 × 2 = 6

  1. Why are symbols used?
  2. Write four intermediate directions.
  3. Why is scale important?

Section D — 2 × 3 = 6

  1. A hospital is east of a school. Where is the school? If map distance is 3 cm and 1 cm = 2 km, find actual distance.
  2. List four things that make a neighbourhood sketch map easy to understand.

Section E — Map Reading = 8

Teacher provides a map. Students identify title, direction, legend, locations, route and distance using scale.
