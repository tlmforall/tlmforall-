Chapter 1 Worksheet

  1. Write two uses of maps.
  2. Name the four main directions.
  3. Write the four intermediate directions.
  4. Explain what a sketch map is.
  5. What is the purpose of a map legend?
  6. If 1 cm = 2 km, what does 6 cm represent?
  7. Design a symbol for a playground.
  8. Draw a simple route from your classroom to the school gate.
  9. Write one example of a place east of your school.
  10. What information should you check before interpreting an unfamiliar map?

Creative Task

Draw a mini map of your neighbourhood with at least five landmarks, a direction indicator and a legend.
