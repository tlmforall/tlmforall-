Chapter Notes — Reading and Making Maps

Maps

A map represents the whole or part of the Earth's surface and helps us locate and understand places.

Directions

The four main directions are North, South, East and West. Intermediate directions include North-East, North-West, South-East and South-West.

Sketch Map

A sketch map is a simple representation showing the relative location of important places or features.

Scale

Scale shows the relationship between map distance and actual ground distance.

Symbols and Legend

Symbols represent features simply. A legend/key explains the symbols used on a map.

Complete Map Reading

Read the title, direction, scale, symbols, labels and locations together.
