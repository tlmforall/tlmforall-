🧭 DIRECTIONS

Learn the four main directions and use them to describe locations.

✨ Inspiration

“Knowing the way helps us find the place.”

Think: If a friend asks where the school is, what information could help?

📖 SCERT Connection

The four main directions are North, South, East and West. Intermediate directions include North-East, North-West, South-East and South-West.

🎮 Which Way?

Hospital is East of the School. Where is the School from the Hospital?

🌍 Google Earth Exploration

Teacher demonstrates a selected location and asks students to identify a direction from one visible landmark to another. Use the map/compass orientation as a support, not as a guessing game.

🤖 TLM AI Challenge

Ask AI: “Give me a direction question.” Then answer by voice or text. AI should increase or reduce difficulty based on the response.

🎫 Exit Ticket

Write one sentence using two places and a direction, e.g. “The park is ___ of the school.”
